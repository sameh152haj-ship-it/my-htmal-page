Green Day – Empowering Communities Through Sustainable Energy

I launched Green Day to bring renewable energy out of the textbook and into young people’s homes and futures. It is where my engineering, teaching and community work meet.

  • Raising awareness about renewable energy and solar power.
  • Promoting energy efficiency and responsible energy consumption.
  • Introducing young people to basic electrical concepts.
  • Developing practical knowledge for independent home maintenance.
  • Introducing career opportunities as Energy Engineers and Energy Technicians.
